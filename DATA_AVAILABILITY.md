# Data availability

The companion dataset is Zenodo Dataset v1.0.0: [10.5281/zenodo.23230371](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23230371). This is a **data DOI**, not a DOI for the GitHub software or manuscript.

The deposit contains 46 data files: 45 original author-derived aggregate TSVs (12 S11, 20 R6 main-panel summaries, and 13 R8.2 supplementary-panel summaries) plus the unchanged DepMap Public 24Q4 common-essential-list CSV. Supporting documentation, provenance, and checksums are also in the archive. The deposited author-derived aggregate tables are under the dataset record's CC BY 4.0 license to the extent the depositors hold the relevant rights; the DepMap CSV remains subject to its original CC BY 4.0 attribution.

The archive is not a full raw-data or gene-level analysis-ready release. It excludes the Activity H5AD, IGVF/BioPlex mixed inputs, full in-vivo source scores, full gene-level external score matrices, donor/sample audit data, workbooks, manuscripts, and formal figure files. Some analyses and figures therefore cannot be independently regenerated from the deposit alone. See the external-input and reproducibility metadata for exact limitations.
